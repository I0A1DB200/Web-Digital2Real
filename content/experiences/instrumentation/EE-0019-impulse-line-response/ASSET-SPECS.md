# EE-0019 asset specifications

## Shared visual constraints

- Same stainless process vessel, side-mounted blue differential-pressure transmitter, three-valve manifold, two impulse lines, local gauge and junction box.
- Same daylight process-plant setting, neutral camera language and 16:9 framing.
- No manufacturer logos, EE/ENV identifiers, editorial titles or navigation burned into images.
- Charts use `REF`, `PV`, `bar` and `t (s)` so the technical evidence remains locale-independent.
- Values are didactic and must not be read as a plant acceptance limit.

## Acceptance matrix

| Asset | Learning moment | Must show | Must not show |
|---|---|---|---|
| ART-001 | Incident Brief | Complete installation and physical interfaces | Fault proof, progress or answer |
| ART-002 | Initial incident | Reference step ahead of delayed PV | Root cause or intervention |
| ART-003 | Investigation | Measurement boundaries in order | A specific vendor topology |
| ART-004 | Investigation | Same manifold and impulse tubing under inspection | Open pressure boundary |
| ART-005 | Acquired diagnosis | Restriction on the physical pressure path | A universal valve or grounding rule |
| ART-006 | Intervention evidence | Isolated test point and controlled conditioning | Work on a pressurized connection |
| ART-007 | Completion | Aligned REF/PV at low, mid and high changes | Claim of a normative response time |
