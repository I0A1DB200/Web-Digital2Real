# EE-0019 — Respuesta lenta por obstrucción en la línea de impulso

ENV-002 Experience for locating a dynamic pressure-response fault in a differential-pressure measurement chain.

## Engineering case

The authored root cause is a partial restriction in one impulse line or its immediate connection. The process reference, local transmitter indication, electronics and engineering scaling remain correct; the restriction damps and delays the pressure change reaching the transmitter and control input.

The case is intentionally distinct from EE-0012 (stable current with incorrect scaling), EE-0017 (intermittent 4–20 mA continuity) and EE-0018 (0–10 V common-reference noise). EE-0019 teaches dynamic boundary testing and physical pressure-path diagnosis.

## Learner flow

preserve symptom -> compare independent reference -> run controlled dynamic test -> inspect manifold and impulse path -> isolate and condition under the installation procedure -> verify low, mid and high response through the complete chain

## Visual and graph coherence

Seven learner-facing RGB PNGs use one installation: the same stainless vessel, blue differential-pressure transmitter, three-valve manifold, two impulse lines, local gauge and junction box. Three designed technical graphics show the lag, the measurement boundaries, the restriction and the multipoint recovery. The generated photographs are illustrative and contain no EE/ENV identifier or manufacturer claim.

The graphs use didactic values and are evidence for the authored case, not production measurements or a universal acceptance limit.
