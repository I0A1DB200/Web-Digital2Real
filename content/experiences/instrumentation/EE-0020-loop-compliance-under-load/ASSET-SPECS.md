# EE-0020 asset specifications

All seven assets are 1672×941 RGB PNGs and keep one visual installation: stainless process vessel, blue two-wire transmitter, 24 VDC source cabinet, terminal enclosure, analog input and test bench.

- `ART-001-loop-station.png` — cover photo; no people; transmitter, source and input path in one view.
- `ART-002-loop-collapse.png` — incident graph; reference remains stable while loop current falls after the load is connected.
- `ART-003-multimeter-boundaries.png` — close-up photo; two digital multimeters at approved current and voltage test points; no people.
- `ART-004-boundary-measurement.png` — boundary diagram for source, wiring, transmitter and analog input; measurement points are explicit.
- `ART-005-commissioning-software.png` — illustrative external commissioning-tool panel showing connected status, current, burden check and voltage margin.
- `ART-006-restored-loop.png` — corrected installation photo; organized supply, wiring, load and input path; no people.
- `ART-007-multipoint-recovery.png` — recovery graph; low, mid and high loaded transitions track through the chain.

The software panel is intentionally illustrative and vendor-neutral. It must not be read as a screenshot of a specific product or as proof of an installed device capability.
